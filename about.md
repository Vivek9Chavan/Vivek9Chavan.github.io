<header class="page-heading"><p class="eyebrow">Background & experience</p><h1>About me<span class="name-dot">.</span></h1><p>Connecting machine learning research with the demands of real-world systems.</p></header>
<div class="about-grid"><div class="prose" markdown="1">

I am a PhD candidate at TU Berlin and a Research Associate at Fraunhofer IPK. My research connects multimodal perception, procedural understanding, and embodied action: how can a system learn from human demonstrations, maintain an understanding of a task, and act reliably as conditions change?

Before moving into machine learning research, I worked on vehicle simulation and engineering with Tata Technologies and Jaguar Land Rover in India and the UK. That experience continues to shape my interest in rigorous evaluation and practical deployment.

### Research & mentorship

At Fraunhofer IPK, I develop computer vision and multimodal learning systems, collaborate across research and industrial projects, and supervise student research. My mentorship includes five Bachelor’s/Master’s theses and nine research internships.

I also contribute to the AI Grid community in Berlin and have served as a reviewer or sub-reviewer for CVPR and ECCV 2026.
